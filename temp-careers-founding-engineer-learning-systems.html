About Gausyn

Gausyn is a personal teacher for every student. It plans a student’s week around their real deadlines, teaches lessons out loud on a shared board, works through problems beside them without giving the answer away, and stays with them from Grade 3 fractions to graduate work.

We are a small team, built in Toronto, and Gausyn is a product of Inquisara. We ship weekly to real students, so what you build this week is in front of someone learning something that matters to them by the next.

The role

Gausyn has to do more than produce a reasonable answer. It has to see a worksheet, ground its help in real worked solutions, place a hint beside the right question, and speak with a teacher’s timing.

You will own the systems that make that true, and the measurements that tell us whether it is true. Better teaching is the goal, and a good demo is not the same thing.

What you will do

  • Build and harden the pipelines behind worksheet understanding and board guidance
  • Chase latency out of the listen, think, speak loop until it feels like conversation
  • Design evaluations that measure teaching quality, not just output plausibility
  • Turn real session transcripts into measurable improvements

What you will bring

  • Experience with speech, vision, or language systems in production
  • Rigor about measured quality over vibes
  • A conviction that pedagogy is an engineering constraint, not a nice-to-have

Nice to have

  • You have worked on real-time voice or other low-latency systems
  • You have built evaluations from scratch for something hard to measure
  • You have taught a subject you love
