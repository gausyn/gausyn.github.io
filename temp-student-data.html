Student data

Last updated 24 August 2026Gausyn, a product of Inquisara

Student data deserves a plainer promise than a privacy policy usually gives. This page is that promise, written for parents, teachers, and the people at a school who have to sign something.

1.Who this is for

This page covers students who use Gausyn through a family account and students who use it through a school, program, or tutoring organisation. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy; where a signed agreement with an institution says something different, that agreement governs for those students.

2.What we mean by student data

Everything a student generates or provides in the course of being taught: their profile and grade level, the subjects they take, their availability and deadlines, the boards they draw on, the worksheets and photographs they upload, their questions in text and voice, and the record of which lessons ran.

3.Our commitments

  • We do not sell student data. Not to anyone, at any price, ever.
  • We do not advertise to students, and we do not share student data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles of students for anything other than teaching them the subject in front of them.
  • Student content is not used to train third-party AI models. We use our AI providers under business terms that exclude customer content from their general model training.
  • Student data belongs to the student, or, for an institutional account, to the institution. We hold it to run the service, not as an asset of our own.
  • We tell you when something goes wrong. If student data is ever exposed, affected accounts and institutions hear it from us.

4.Homework you upload

There are two paths, and they behave differently on purpose.

Inside a live session, a worksheet or photo you upload is loaded onto the board and kept with that board so your work and the tutor's hints stay where you left them.

Attached ahead of a session, from the daily check-in for example, the file is read once to produce a short summary of the topic and problem type, that summary is kept so the tutor knows roughly what is coming, and the file itself is discarded rather than stored. It is why Gausyn asks you to re-upload the sheet when the session actually starts.

5.Who processes it

Gausyn runs on a small number of service providers, each doing one job: hosting and database, AI inference, speech transcription and synthesis, payment processing, and error monitoring. Institutions can request the current list of sub-processors, including where each operates, before signing.

We do not add a sub-processor that handles student content without updating that list.

6.Retention and deletion

We keep student data while the account is open, because continuity is the point of a teacher who knows you. When an account is deleted, its personal information and session content are removed within 30 days.

A parent, guardian, or institution can request export or deletion at any time, and we will not charge for it or make the product worse in response.

7.For schools and districts

We can sign a data protection agreement, complete a security questionnaire, and provide a sub-processor list and retention schedule. For institutional accounts we can also scope who at the institution may see a student's session content, and we default to the narrowest option that still lets teachers do their job.

Gausyn is in private testing, so we would rather tell you now what we do not yet have: a completed third-party security audit, a published VPAT, formal certification. Better that than letting you find out during procurement.

8.How this maps to the law

Inquisara is Canadian and handles personal information under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy law. Where we serve students in other jurisdictions, we work to the standards those regimes expect of an education provider, including parental consent requirements of the kind COPPA sets for younger children, the school-official model FERPA describes for institutional data, and GDPR rights of access, correction, portability, and erasure.

We are not going to claim certifications we do not hold. If a specific compliance obligation is blocking you, ask us directly and we will answer specifically.
