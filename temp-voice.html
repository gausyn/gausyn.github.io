Product · Voice

A lesson you can talk to.

The fastest interface for confusion is your own voice. Gausyn listens when you ask it to, speaks like a teacher, and never minds being interrupted.

“Explain that again” “En français?” “Give me a harder one”
01

The mic is yours

Gausyn only listens when you click the microphone. No always-on listening, no hot words. What you say becomes part of the lesson; what you don't say stays in the room.

02

Freeform, when you want flow

Turn on Freeform and the conversation breathes: Gausyn listens after it finishes speaking and picks up when you stop. It is the closest thing to sitting next to someone.

03

Interrupt mid-sentence

Click the mic or just type while it is talking — it stops and listens. A teacher you cannot interrupt is a lecture, and we are not building lectures.

04

Typing is always equal

Every step works silently too. In a library, on a bus, at 2 a.m. with the house asleep — the chat panel does everything the voice does.

Confusion speaks. Now something listens.
