Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026Gausyn, a product of Inquisara

Gausyn is a tutor for students, and a great deal of what a student tells a tutor is personal. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we refuse to do with it.

About this document

Gausyn is in private testing. This document is a working draft, prepared with the assistance of AI and reviewed by our team, and it will be finalised with counsel before Gausyn opens to the public. If anything here matters to a decision you are making, write to us and we will answer plainly.

1.Who we are

Gausyn is built and operated by Inquisara, a company based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. When this policy says we or us, it means Inquisara. When it says you, it means the person using Gausyn, or the parent, guardian, or school acting on their behalf.

Inquisara is the controller of the personal information described here.

2.What we collect

We collect four kinds of information, and nothing beyond what the product needs to work.

Account information

Your email address and password credentials, handled by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password in readable form.

Your learning profile

What you tell us during onboarding and in settings: your name, your grade or year, the subjects and courses you are taking, your goals, how you say you learn best, how often you want to study, and the weekly windows when you are available. This is what lets Gausyn plan a week that fits your life rather than an average one.

Session content

What happens in a lesson: what you write and draw on the board, the worksheets and photographs you upload, your typed messages, your spoken questions, and the tutor's replies. Snapshots of the visible board are sent with each exchange so the tutor can see what you are actually working on.

Usage and technical information

Which lessons ran and for how long, how much of your monthly allowance has been used, and standard technical records such as browser type, device type, approximate region, and error logs.

Payments are handled by our payment processor. We receive a record that a payment succeeded and which plan it was for. We never receive or store your card number.

3.How we use it

  • To teach: to run a session, place hints beside the right problem, and check your working against the problem's solution.
  • To plan: to build and reschedule your week inside the availability you gave us, and to track the deadlines you asked us to track.
  • To remember: so a board you leave and come back to is exactly as you left it.
  • To bill: to apply your plan's monthly allowance and to process subscription payments.
  • To keep the service working and safe: diagnosing faults, preventing abuse, and meeting our legal obligations.
  • To improve Gausyn: understanding, in aggregate, which parts of the product help students and which do not.

4.AI processing

Gausyn's tutoring is delivered by large language models, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech services operated by third-party AI providers. To answer you, we send that provider the relevant part of your session: your message, recent conversation, a snapshot of the visible board, and the reference solutions worked out for your worksheet.

We use these providers under their business terms, which are the terms that exclude customer content from being used to train their general models. We ask them to process your content only to return a reply to you.

Voice is optional. If you never enable the microphone, no audio ever leaves your device.

5.What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never will.
  • We do not show advertising in Gausyn, and we do not share student data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not build profiles of students for any purpose other than teaching them.
  • We do not keep the homework files you upload. A photograph or PDF attached outside a live session is read once for a short summary of the topic, and the file itself is discarded rather than stored.

6.Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make Gausyn run, and only as far as their job requires:

  • Hosting and database — storing your account, lessons, and boards.
  • AI providers — generating the tutor's replies, transcription, and speech.
  • Payment processing — subscriptions and receipts.
  • Error and performance monitoring — so we find faults before you report them.

We may also disclose information where the law genuinely requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If we are ever compelled to hand over student data, we will tell the affected accounts unless we are legally forbidden from doing so.

If Inquisara is ever acquired or merged, personal information may transfer as part of that transaction. The commitments in this policy would travel with it, and we would give notice before anything changed.

7.How long we keep it

We keep your account, learning profile, lessons, and board content for as long as your account is open, because that continuity is the product: a teacher who forgets you every session is not much of a teacher.

Uploaded files attached ahead of a session are summarised and deleted, as described above. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and session content within 30 days, except for records we are required to keep for tax, accounting, or legal reasons, and aggregate statistics that no longer identify anyone.

8.Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Data is stored with row-level access rules so one account cannot read another's. Access by our team is limited to the people who need it to operate and support the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires, and we will tell you what actually happened.

9.Children and parents

Gausyn is built for students, including students under the age of majority. Where a child is below the age at which they can consent for themselves, an account must be created and consented to by a parent, guardian, or a school acting under its own authority.

Parents and guardians may ask us at any time to show them what we hold about their child, correct it, or delete it. Write to us from the address on the account and we will act on it.

We do not knowingly collect more from a child than the teaching requires, and we do not use student data for advertising or profiling of any kind.

10.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • give you a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information and close your account;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw a consent you gave.

Email us and we will respond within 30 days. We will never charge you for exercising a right or make the service worse because you did.

11.Where data is processed

Inquisara is Canadian. Our hosting, AI, and payment providers operate in Canada, the United States, and the European Union, so your information may be processed outside your own country and will be subject to the laws of those countries. Where transfers require a legal mechanism such as standard contractual clauses, we rely on those our providers have in place.

12.Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle student information, we will tell account holders directly rather than quietly editing the page.
