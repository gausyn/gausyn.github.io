Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026gausyn, a product of Inquisara

gausyn teaches students, and a great deal of what a student tells a teacher is personal. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we refuse to do with it.

gausyn is in private testing and this document is a working draft. It will be settled with a lawyer before gausyn opens to the public. If anything here matters to a decision you are making, write to us and we will answer plainly.

1.Who we are

gausyn is built and run by Inquisara, a company based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. When this policy says we or us, it means Inquisara. When it says you, it means the person using gausyn, or the parent or guardian who made the account for their child.

Inquisara decides how the personal information described here is used, and is responsible for it.

2.The short version

  • We collect what gausyn needs to teach: an email address, a first name, a school level, and what happens in lessons.
  • We ask your age when you sign up and keep only your age group, never your age or a date of birth.
  • A child under 13 can only use gausyn through an account that a parent or guardian makes and agrees to.
  • gausyn's teaching is produced by AI models run by other companies. We send them only what a reply needs, and they may not use it to train their models.
  • We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or share student data with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, yourself, at any time, in Settings.

3.Who can have an account

Your own account. You must be 13 or older. If you are 13 to 17, you confirm when you sign up that a parent or guardian knows you are using gausyn and agrees. We do not make accounts for anyone who tells us they are under 13.

An account for your child. A parent or legal guardian, 18 or older, can make an account for their child of any school age. The account belongs to the parent or guardian, who confirms who they are and gives permission for us to collect and use the child's information as this policy describes. Section 10 is written for them.

4.What we collect

Only what the product needs to work.

Your account

  • Your email address, and your password in a scrambled form kept by our sign-in provider. We never see your password.
  • Whether the account is your own or one you made for your child.
  • For your own account, your age group: 13 to 17, or 18 and over. We ask your age once and keep only the group.
  • What you agreed to when you signed up, and when: these terms and this policy, and, where it applies, that a parent or guardian agrees or gives permission.

Your learning profile

What you tell us when setting up and in Settings: a first name (on a child's account, the child's first name), level or grade, subjects and courses, goals, how you like to be taught, how often you want to learn, the times you are free, and your time zone.

What you do in gausyn

  • Lessons on the whiteboard. What you write, draw, type and say during a lesson is sent to our servers so gausyn can answer, together with a picture of the visible board and the worksheet pages or photos you have loaded onto it. The board itself is saved in your own browser for a short time, so you can come back to it. We do not store boards on our servers.
  • Your calendar. The lessons planned for you, when they ran, and a one-line summary of how each went. When a lesson ends, the recent conversation from it is read once to write that summary and to update what gausyn remembers about your learning. The conversation itself is not kept.
  • Learning conversations. Saved to your account so you can return to them, until you delete them.
  • Projects. Your drafts, code, the files you upload and the pictures gausyn makes for you are saved to your account, until you delete them.
  • Material you attach ahead of a lesson. The file is read once to write a short summary of the topic. The summary is kept. The file is not.
  • What gausyn remembers. Short notes about how you learn, such as what helped last time, a goal you mentioned, or where a lesson stopped. You can read them and make gausyn forget any of them in Settings, under Memory. gausyn is built not to keep notes about health, religion, family circumstances or similar private matters.
  • Your voice. When you speak to gausyn, the recording is sent to be turned into text and is then discarded. Only the text stays, as part of the conversation. If you never use the microphone, no audio leaves your device.
  • Learning with friends. Your friend code, the friends you add, and the spaces you make or join. In a space, the people in it see your first name, the board, what you write in the chat, and hear your gausyn. Voice and video calls go between the browsers of the people in the space. We do not record them.

Your plan

Which plan you are on, how much of its limits you have used (each request is counted with its kind and its cost), and, if you pay, the identifiers and payment status our payment processor gives us. We never receive or store your card number.

Technical information

Standard server records, kept for a limited time by our hosting providers: internet address, browser and device type, dates and times, and error reports.

On your device

A cookie that keeps you signed in, and a few small settings saved in your browser, such as light or dark mode. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

5.How we use it

  • To teach. To run a lesson, answer your questions, draw and speak, and pitch everything at your level.
  • To plan. To build your week inside the times you gave us, and to keep track of the deadlines you asked us to track.
  • To remember. So gausyn can pick up where you left off instead of starting from nothing every time.
  • To run your plan. To apply your plan's limits and to take subscription payments.
  • To keep gausyn working and safe. To find faults, prevent abuse, and meet our legal obligations.
  • To improve gausyn. To understand, in total and without singling anyone out, which parts of the product help students and which do not.

Where the law asks for a legal basis: we use your information to provide the service you asked for, with your consent (or your parent's or guardian's), to meet legal duties, and for our legitimate interest in keeping gausyn safe and making it better.

6.AI processing

gausyn is a computer program, not a person. Its replies, drawings and voice are produced by AI models run by other companies. To answer you, we send those companies the part of your session that the reply needs.

  • OpenAI produces gausyn's replies and drawings and turns speech into text. It receives your message, the recent conversation, a picture of the visible board, the pages or photos you loaded, the basics of your profile (first name, level, subjects), what gausyn remembers about how you learn, and, when you speak, the recording. Under the terms we use, OpenAI does not use this content to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse. Recordings sent for turning into text are not kept.
  • ElevenLabs produces gausyn's voice. It receives only the words gausyn is about to say, as text, which can include your first name. It never receives your voice. We have set our account so that this text is not used to train its models.

gausyn's voice is computer-generated, not a human voice. At times it may be produced by OpenAI instead of ElevenLabs, on the same terms as above.

gausyn makes no decision about you with legal or similarly serious effect. It does not grade, rank or report on students.

7.What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never will.
  • We do not show advertising in gausyn, and we do not share student data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not build profiles of students for any purpose other than teaching them.
  • We do not use what students write, draw or say to train AI models, and the companies we use may not either.
  • We do not record voice or video calls between friends.

8.Who we share it with

Only the companies that make gausyn run, and only as far as their job requires:

  • Supabase. Sign-in, the database, and stored project files.
  • Vercel and Railway. Hosting for the app and for the whiteboard's server.
  • OpenAI and ElevenLabs. Replies, drawings, speech to text, and the voice, as section 6 describes.
  • Stripe. Subscriptions, payments and receipts.
  • An email delivery service. The emails gausyn sends you, such as confirming your address or choosing a new password.

Two smaller things happen in your browser. To show mathematics and to read scanned worksheets, gausyn loads open-source code from public content networks (Cloudflare and jsDelivr). To connect a call between friends, your browser asks Google's public connection service for its own internet address. These see your device's internet address, not your work.

The people in a space you join see what section 4 lists under learning with friends.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If we are ever made to hand over student data, we will tell the affected accounts unless the law forbids it.

If Inquisara is ever bought or merged, personal information may pass to the new owner as part of that. The commitments in this policy would go with it, and we would give notice before anything changed.

9.How long we keep it

We keep your account, profile, lessons, conversations and projects for as long as your account is open, because a teacher who forgets you every time is not much of a teacher.

  • Delete one thing. You can delete a Learning conversation, a project or a file, and make gausyn forget a note, at any time.
  • Start again. Settings, Account, has "Reset my learning data", which clears your lessons, conversations, memory and projects and keeps the account.
  • Delete everything. Settings, Account, has "Delete my account". It cancels any paid plan and removes your profile, lessons, conversations, memory, projects, files, friends and spaces, and the login itself, straight away. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as payment records, and totals that no longer identify anyone.

Our database provider keeps backups for up to 7 days to recover from faults; anything deleted is gone from them once they expire. Voice recordings and files attached ahead of a lesson are not kept, as section 4 describes. The companies in section 8 keep their own short-lived records, such as OpenAI's 30 days.

10.Children and parents

This section is for parents and guardians.

Children under 13

A child under 13 cannot make an account. If someone tells us at sign-up that they are under 13, we do not make an account and we ask for nothing further: no name, no email address. A parent or guardian can make an account for the child instead. If we learn that a child under 13 is using gausyn without one, we close the account and delete what is in it.

An account you make for your child

The account is yours, under your email address. When you make it, you confirm that you are 18 or older and the child's parent or legal guardian, and you give permission for us to collect and use your child's information as this policy describes. We keep a record of that and its date.

What we collect from your child is what section 4 lists: a first name, a school level, and what they write, draw, say, upload and make while learning. We do not ask a child for a surname, an address, a phone number or a photograph, and we do not require more information than taking part needs.

Who receives it is listed in sections 6 and 8. Nobody else does, and none of it is used for advertising.

What you can do, at any time

  • See it. Sign in to the account: the lessons, conversations, projects and what gausyn remembers are all there. Or ask us for a copy.
  • Correct it. In Settings, or by writing to us.
  • Delete it, and stop any further collection. Settings, Account, "Delete my account", or write to us and we will do it.

We may need to check that a request about a child really comes from the parent or guardian on the account before acting on it.

Learning with friends

Collaborate lets your child add friends by a code and learn with them in a shared space, with chat and, if they turn it on, voice and video. Only people your child has added, or who were given the space's invitation link, can be in a space. Please decide with your child who they add.

Ages 13 to 17

A student of 13 to 17 may make their own account after confirming that a parent or guardian knows and agrees. Parents and guardians of these students may ask us at any time to show, correct or delete what we hold. Write to us from the account's address, or with enough detail for us to find the account.

11.Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Data is stored with access rules so that one account cannot read another's. Access by our team is limited to the people who need it to run and support the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires, and we will tell you what actually happened.

12.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • give you a copy of it in a form you can take elsewhere;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information and close your account;
  • limit or object to certain uses, and withdraw a consent you gave.

Email us and we will answer within 30 days. We never charge for this, and we never make the service worse because you asked. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the privacy regulator where you live. In Canada that is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

13.Where data is processed

Inquisara is Canadian. The companies in section 8 process data in the United States and in other countries where they operate, so your information may be processed outside your own country and be subject to the laws there. Where a transfer needs a legal mechanism, such as standard contractual clauses, we rely on the ones our providers have in place.

14.Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we handle student information in a way that matters, we will tell account holders directly, and ask for agreement again where the law requires it, rather than quietly editing the page.
