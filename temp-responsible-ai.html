Responsible AI

Last updated 19 October 2026gausyn, a product of Inquisara

gausyn teaches children and teenagers, on work many of them will be graded on, using AI models made by other companies. That deserves plain answers: what gausyn is, how it is kept honest, where it can still be wrong, how it keeps students safe, and what we have refused to build.

gausyn is in private testing and this page describes it as it is today. It will be reviewed with a lawyer, with the Privacy Policy and the Terms, before gausyn opens to the public. If anything here matters to a decision you are making, write to us and we will answer plainly.

1.What gausyn is

gausyn is a computer program that teaches. It plans lessons, teaches them out loud on a whiteboard, draws, answers questions in Learning and helps with Projects. Its replies, drawings and voice are produced by AI models run by other companies: one for replies, drawings and turning speech into text, and another for the voice. At times a second voice, from the first company, is used instead.

gausyn is not a person and never claims to be one. If a student asks whether it is a person, it says plainly that it is a computer program that teaches. Its voice is computer-generated, and when the second voice is speaking, the app says so on screen.

It is a learning tool. It is not a school, an examiner, or a replacement for a teacher or a parent.

2.How gausyn is kept honest

Four design decisions do most of the work.

  • It works the worksheet first. When a worksheet is loaded onto the board, gausyn first solves every problem on it to make a reference solution, then checks the student's steps against that rather than improvising.
  • Its writing on the page is placed by rules. Where a hint goes is worked out from the worksheet's own layout: the empty space beside the question it belongs to. It never goes over printed text or over the student's own writing. The AI model never chooses the position.
  • It does not do the homework. On a student's own worksheet, gausyn gives a hint, a step or a question, never the finished answer. It does not fill in a blank or pick a multiple-choice answer.
  • It stays on learning. It refuses, kindly, requests that are harmful, dangerous, sexual or deceptive, and keeps everything suitable for the student's age.

In a planned lesson, gausyn teaches as a teacher would: it explains, asks check questions, and if the student does not answer, explains the answer itself before moving on.

Pictures are made with the same care. Every request for a picture is wrapped in a fixed description of a simple school diagram, with no real people, photorealistic faces or violence, and is made with the stricter of the two safety settings the picture service offers. The AI model's own words never reach the picture service unchanged.

3.Where it can be wrong

All of this reduces mistakes. None of it removes them.

  • AI models can be confidently wrong, especially on long reasoning with many steps, on unusual notation, and on diagrams they cannot read clearly.
  • Reading handwriting and scanned pages is imperfect. One misread digit can send a hint the wrong way.
  • The reference solution is made by an AI model too. If it is wrong, gausyn's checks are wrong with it.
  • Drawings can have mistakes in their labels, numbers or proportions.
  • gausyn can reflect biases in the models it is built on, for example in the people and places it invents for word problems.

So treat gausyn as a patient teacher who is usually right and sometimes not, rather than as an answer key. Check anything that will be graded, and tell us when it gets something wrong. Our Terms of Service say the same.

4.Keeping students safe

Many people who use gausyn are under 18, and some are under 13, on an account a parent or guardian made for them. That shapes what gausyn is allowed to do.

  • When a student is not safe. If a student says or hints that they might hurt themselves, that someone is hurting them, or that they or someone else is in danger, gausyn stops teaching for that reply. It tells them it is glad they said so and that it matters, that it is not a person and cannot help the way a person can, and asks them to tell a parent, a teacher or another adult they trust that day. It gives them the numbers: 911 or the local emergency number if they may be in danger right now, 988 by call or text in Canada and the United States, and in Canada, Kids Help Phone at 1-800-668-6868 or by texting CONNECT to 686868. It does not ask for details or try to counsel them, and it goes back to the lesson only if the student asks.
  • gausyn alerts nobody. No one watches conversations as they happen, and gausyn does not tell parents, schools or us when a student says something like this. gausyn is not a crisis or mental-health service. A child in danger needs a person.
  • What gausyn remembers is short notes about how a student learns. It is built not to keep notes about health, religion, family circumstances or similar private matters, and the student or parent can read and delete every note in Settings, under Memory.
  • Learning with friends happens only with people a student adds by their friend code, or who were given a space's invitation link. Calls are not recorded.
  • Pictures are made with the safety framing described in section 2.

5.Integrity by design

The easiest teaching program to build is one that does the homework. We chose not to build that.

On a worksheet, gausyn guides and does not fill in answers. In Learning it explains and works through examples, and in Projects it builds things with the student: drafts, code and designs. Whether that kind of help is allowed for a piece of schoolwork is up to the student's school. Handing in what gausyn produced as one's own unaided work, where the school forbids it, breaks our Terms of Service as well as the school's rules.

If you find a way to make gausyn fill in a worksheet's answers, that is a bug, and we would like to hear about it.

6.People, not automatic decisions

  • gausyn makes no decision about a student with legal or similarly serious effect. It does not give marks that count, rank students, or report on them to anyone. When it says whether an answer is right, that is teaching, not a grade.
  • We do not read students' conversations as a matter of course. Our servers' logs record sizes and errors, not what students say or write. When an account holder reports a problem, we look at what they show us, or, with their permission, at the account, and we fix the instructions, the rules or the product.
  • People at Inquisara decide what gausyn is allowed to do, and are responsible for it.

7.AI models and your data

To answer a student, we send the AI companies only the part of the session that the reply needs. Under the terms we use, our AI model provider does not use it to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse; recordings sent to be turned into text are not kept. Our voice provider receives only the words gausyn is about to say, and our account is set so that they are not used to train its models. We do not train AI models on what students write, draw or say either.

The full details are in the Privacy Policy and on the Student data page.

8.What we will not build

  • We will not sell student data, show advertising in gausyn, or fund gausyn with advertising to children.
  • We will not build streaks, points, leaderboards or other tricks meant to keep a child on gausyn longer than learning needs. A session that ends early because the student understood is a success.
  • We will not present gausyn as a person, or let it claim to be one.
  • We will not add features whose main use is handing in work a student did not do.
  • We will not analyse students' faces or voices to identify them or to judge their feelings.
  • We will not use a student's work, words or struggles in our marketing.

9.Reporting a problem

If gausyn taught something wrong, said something inappropriate, or behaved in a way that worries you, tell us. Include the subject, roughly when it happened, and what it said if you can.
