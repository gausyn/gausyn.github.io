1.Recent incidents

No incidents recorded. Gausyn is in private testing with a small group of students, and this page will carry a dated entry for every outage or degradation once we are serving people who depend on us.

2.Planned maintenance

None scheduled. While Gausyn is in private testing we deploy changes continuously and occasionally take short maintenance windows without notice. Once there are timetabled lessons on the calendar, planned work will be announced here and by email to affected accounts in advance.

3.Service levels

We do not publish an uptime commitment during private testing, and our Terms of Service reflect that. Schools and programs that need a written service level should raise it with us before signing; we would rather agree to something we can keep than advertise a number we cannot.

4.Report an outage

If Gausyn is down for you and this page says otherwise, that is worth knowing. Tell us what you were doing and roughly when.
