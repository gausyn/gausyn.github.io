1.What this page checks

The first line above is checked live: this page asks gausyn's lesson server whether it is up, and shows the answer and the time. The other lines are services gausyn depends on, run by other companies. Their own status pages say whether they are working.

Some problems cannot be seen from here, for example one on a single device or in one school's network. If the check says gausyn is not answering, try your own connection first. If gausyn is not working for you and this page says it is up, please tell us.

2.Recent incidents

gausyn is in private testing, and we have not kept a public log of incidents yet. From the public launch, every outage or slowdown that affects students will get a dated entry here: what happened, for how long, and what we changed.

3.Updates and maintenance

We release improvements often, usually without any break. If a lesson stops answering for a moment, reload the page: the board is saved in your browser. Once students depend on gausyn for booked lessons, planned work that interrupts it will be announced here in advance, and by email to the accounts it affects.

4.Service levels

We do not promise a level of availability while gausyn is in private testing, as our Terms of Service say. A school or program that needs a written commitment should raise it with us before relying on gausyn. We would rather agree to something we can keep than advertise a number we cannot.

5.Report an outage

If gausyn is not working for you, tell us what you were doing and roughly when.
