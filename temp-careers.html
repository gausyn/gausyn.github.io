1.About gausyn

gausyn plans a student’s week around their real deadlines, teaches lessons out loud on a shared board, and works through problems beside them on their own worksheet without giving the answer away. It stays with them from Grade 3 fractions to graduate work.

We are a small team, and gausyn is a product of Inquisara. gausyn is in private testing with real students, and we ship every week, so what you build is in front of someone learning something that matters to them soon after.

3.How we work

  • We are a small team. Everyone builds, and everyone hears from students.
  • We ship every week. Work goes from an idea to a real student’s screen in days.
  • We work remotely across the US and Canada.
  • We write things down, so that decisions and what we learned are easy to find later.
  • We judge our work by what students learn, not by how it looks in a demo.
  • Students trust us with their work, and many of them are children. Privacy and safety are part of every feature from the start.
  • We give and take feedback that is kind, specific and useful.

4.What every role includes

  • A full-time, permanent position
  • Remote work from anywhere in the US or Canada
  • A salary and an equity stake, both shown with each role
  • Real ownership: as one of the first people here, you will shape the product, the team and how we work
  • Time with students: you will see how students use what you build, with their permission

We will go through benefits, equipment and your start date with you in the first conversation.

5.How to apply

Email your resume to hello@gausyn.com, with the role in the subject line. In the email, include:

  • A few short points on why you are right for the role: the experience, projects and skills that matter most
  • Links to anything you have built, written or taught
  • The name of anyone who referred you
  • When you could start

If there is something unusual about you, mention it. The clearer and shorter, the better.
