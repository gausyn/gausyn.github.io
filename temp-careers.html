Help build the teacher everyone deserved.

We are a small team in Toronto shipping weekly to real students. The work is early, the surface area is enormous, and every feature lands in front of someone learning something that matters to them.

Toronto · full time · founding team

Founding Full-Stack Engineer

Own features end to end: the calendar that plans a student's week, the board they write on, the session lifecycle underneath it all.

What you will do

  • Ship weekly across a Next.js / TypeScript frontend and a Node backend
  • Build real-time canvas interactions — ink, worksheets, annotations — that feel effortless
  • Own Postgres schemas, auth, and the server-authoritative session clock
  • Watch students use what you built, then make it better that week

You might be

  • A few years into building web products people actually use, or with a portfolio that says the same thing louder
  • Comfortable owning a feature from database row to pixel
  • Happiest when the metric is a student saying “oh, I get it now”

Founding Engineer, Learning Systems

The systems that let Gausyn see a worksheet, ground its help in real worked solutions, place a hint beside the right question, and speak with a teacher's timing.

What you will do

  • Build and harden the pipelines behind worksheet understanding and board guidance
  • Chase latency out of the listen–think–speak loop until it feels like conversation
  • Design evaluations that measure teaching quality, not just output plausibility
  • Turn real session transcripts into measurable improvements

You might be

  • Experienced with speech, vision, or language systems in production, Python and PyTorch in hand
  • Rigorous about measured quality over vibes
  • Convinced that pedagogy is an engineering constraint, not a nice-to-have

Learning Designer, Curriculum

Decide how Gausyn explains, hints, and assesses — from Grade 3 fractions to university mechanics — and build the lesson and worksheet bank it teaches from.

What you will do

  • Author lesson sequences and paired practice worksheets across levels
  • Define what a good hint is, subject by subject, and encode it
  • Review real sessions and mark where the teaching failed the student
  • Shape how progress and understanding get measured

You might be

  • A teacher or tutor of real students for years, with curriculum you are proud of
  • Opinionated about why explanations fail, with receipts
  • Excited to teach one student well, a million times
