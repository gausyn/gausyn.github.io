Company · Research

How teaching actually lands.

Gausyn is a running experiment in one question: what makes an explanation work for this student, right now? We study it and build what we learn.

01

Where explanations fail

The same idea can be drawn, derived, or told as a story. We study which route lands for which learner — and how quickly a teacher should abandon a route that is not working.

02

Help in the right place

A hint beside the question beats the same hint in a chat window. We measure how placement, timing, and length change whether a student uses help or ignores it.

03

The rhythm of spoken lessons

When to pause, when to check, when to let silence do the teaching. Spoken lessons have a tempo, and getting it wrong is the difference between a conversation and a lecture.

04

Memory of the learner

What is worth remembering about how someone learns — and how a record of every explanation that ever worked on you should shape the next one.

Teachers and researchers who want to study this with us: gausyninc@gmail.com.
