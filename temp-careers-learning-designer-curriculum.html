About Gausyn

Gausyn is a personal teacher for every student. It plans a student’s week around their real deadlines, teaches lessons out loud on a shared board, works through problems beside them without giving the answer away, and stays with them from Grade 3 fractions to graduate work.

We are a small team, and Gausyn is a product of Inquisara. We ship weekly to real students, so what you build this week is in front of someone learning something that matters to them by the next.

The role

You will decide how Gausyn explains, hints, and assesses, and build the lesson and worksheet bank it teaches from, from Grade 3 fractions to university mechanics.

This is a teacher’s job at a different scale. You will read real sessions, find where the teaching failed the student, and change what Gausyn does next time for everyone.

What you will do

  • Author lesson sequences and paired practice worksheets across levels
  • Define what a good hint is, subject by subject, and encode it
  • Review real sessions and mark where the teaching failed the student
  • Shape how progress and understanding get measured

What you will bring

  • Years of teaching real students, with curriculum you are proud of
  • Opinions about why explanations fail, with receipts
  • Excitement about teaching one student well, a million times

Nice to have

  • You have taught more than one subject or level
  • You have written materials other teachers use
  • You are comfortable reading transcripts and working with engineers
