Learning with friends, on one board

The Gausyn team · October 2026 · 1 min read

People have always learned in groups, usually around one kitchen table and one tired textbook. Collaborate is that table, for friends who are not in the same room.

A space holds a study group. Inside it, the board is shared in real time, with named cursors and everyone’s handwriting in their own colour. You can talk over voice or video, and react without interrupting anyone.

Everyone keeps their own teacher

Everyone in a space has their own Gausyn on their own tab, so one person’s question does not take over the room. When a friend starts a lesson you get a note with a Join button. Nobody is moved, and you decide whether to go. If you do, their Gausyn answers the whole group by name.

A room of your own

Not everything should go through a teacher, so there is a Friends room, and Gausyn never reads it. It is for the jokes, the plans, and the “wait, did you get that too?” that make studying with friends worth doing.

Add friends by email or with a friend code, see which space each one is in right now, and answer invites from the top of Collaborate.
