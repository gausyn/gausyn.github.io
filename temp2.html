Chemistry · Reaction rates Teacher mode · 41:08 left
Page 1 of 6

What a rate actually measures

  • How much of something appears or disappears, per unit of time.
  • Always a change divided by an interval, never a single reading.
  • The sign tells you which side of the arrow you are standing on.
rate = Δ[A] / Δt
Page 2 of 6

Why concentration changes it

  • More particles in the same space means more collisions each second.
  • More collisions means more of them clear the energy barrier.
  • This is why rate usually falls off as a reaction proceeds.
rate = k[A]m[B]n
Page 3 of 6

Reading it off a graph

  • The slope of the tangent at any point is the rate at that instant.
  • A steep early curve flattening out is the classic shape.
  • Flat means finished, or something ran out.
slope → instantaneous rate
