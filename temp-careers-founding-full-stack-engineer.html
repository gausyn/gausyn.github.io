1.About the role

gausyn is one product with many surfaces: a calendar that plans a student’s week around their real deadlines, a whiteboard where lessons are taught out loud and worksheets are worked through, conversations with a teacher for each subject, shared spaces where friends learn together, and Projects, where students build apps, presentations, essays and 3D parts with gausyn beside them.

As a founding full-stack engineer you will build across all of it, from the database row to the pixel. The loop is short: you build something, you watch a student use it, and you make it better the same week. There is no handoff, and nobody else to wait for.

2.What you will do

  • Ship every week across the web app, the board and the server behind them
  • Build the real-time board: ink, shapes, worksheets and annotations that feel effortless with a mouse, a pen on a tablet, or a finger on a phone
  • Own the lesson session from start to finish: the session clock, joining and rejoining, saving every board, and picking up exactly where a student left off
  • Build the calendar and planner that turn a student’s deadlines into a week of sessions they can move around
  • Design database schemas, access rules and sign-in so that every student’s work stays theirs
  • Build shared spaces for learning with friends: presence, a shared board and chat, and calls between friends
  • Build file handling end to end: uploads of worksheets, notes and photos, storage, the trash, and the safety check every file passes
  • Build plans and billing, usage limits that reset every 5 hours and every week, and the screens that explain them plainly
  • Keep the product fast and dependable: measure load times, catch errors before students do, and fix them
  • Watch real sessions, with permission, and turn what you see into the next week’s work

3.What we are looking for

  • Several years building web products that real people use, or a portfolio that shows the same
  • Strong skills across a modern web stack: a typed language, component-based interfaces, a server and a relational database
  • Comfort owning a feature from the database to the pixel, including the parts nobody asked for
  • Experience with real-time features: live updates, presence or collaborative editing
  • Care for interface details, from how a stroke feels under a pen to how a page loads on a slow connection
  • Sound judgment about security and privacy, because many of our students are children
  • Able to lead your own work with little guidance in a team that moves fast
  • Writes clearly and concisely, in code and in words
  • A taste for small, finished things over large, unfinished ones
  • Happiest when the measure of success is a student saying “oh, I get it now”

4.Nice to have

  • You have built drawing, canvas or pen-and-ink interfaces
  • You have worked with audio or video in the browser
  • You have built payments, subscriptions or usage metering
  • You have been early at a small company and helped it grow
  • You have worked on a product for students, parents or schools
  • You have taught someone something, even informally
  • You can point to a product that is a joy to use and say exactly why
