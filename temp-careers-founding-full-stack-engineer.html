About Gausyn

Gausyn is a personal teacher for every student. It plans a student’s week around their real deadlines, teaches lessons out loud on a shared board, works through problems beside them without giving the answer away, and stays with them from Grade 3 fractions to graduate work.

We are a small team, and Gausyn is a product of Inquisara. We ship weekly to real students, so what you build this week is in front of someone learning something that matters to them by the next.

The role

You will be one of the first engineers at Gausyn, and almost everything a student touches is yours to shape: the calendar that plans their week, the board they write on, and the session lifecycle underneath it all.

The loop is short. You build something, you watch a student use it, and you make it better the same week. There is no handoff, and nobody else to wait for.

What you will do

  • Ship weekly across the web app and the backend
  • Build real-time canvas interactions, ink, worksheets, annotations, that feel effortless
  • Own database schemas, auth, and the server-authoritative session clock
  • Watch students use what you built, then make it better that week

What you will bring

  • A few years of building web products people actually use, or a portfolio that says the same thing louder
  • Comfort owning a feature from database row to pixel
  • A taste for small, finished things over large, unfinished ones
  • Being happiest when the metric is a student saying “oh, I get it now”

Nice to have

  • You have built real-time or drawing interfaces before
  • You have been early at a small team
  • You have taught someone something, even informally
