About

The teacher everyone deserved.

We are building the teacher we wish every student had. Patient, endlessly available, and willing to explain it again in whatever way finally makes sense.

Why we are building this.

Somewhere tonight, a student is sitting alone with a question no one is there to answer. Not because they cannot learn, but because the right teacher, the patient one, the one who explains it in the way that finally makes sense, has not yet crossed their path.

We have watched this happen up close for years. Between us we have taught physics, mathematics, and computer science to hundreds of students, written university course material, built curricula, run departments, and sat through the long office hours where the real teaching actually happens. The pattern almost never changed. Capable students struggled not because the material was beyond them, but because the explanation, the pace, the language, or the example in front of them did not match the way their mind works.

A great teacher fixes that in seconds. They notice the confusion, throw the explanation away, and try a different one. But teachers have limited hours, private tutoring is expensive, and almost every resource online hands the same fixed explanation to every learner who arrives.

Learning should go deep. Not answers handed over, but understanding built.

What we believe.

01

The lesson should adapt, not the student

Every other product asks the learner to bend to a fixed textbook, video, or curriculum. It should be the other way around. The teaching should reassemble itself around the person in front of it.

02

Understanding should stay

A finished assignment is worth very little. An idea that clicks and holds for the rest of someone's life is worth almost everything. We build for the second one, even when it is slower.

03

Access should not depend on luck

Not on the country you were born in, the language you speak at home, the school you were assigned to, or what your family can afford. This only matters if it reaches everyone.

Who we are.

We are brothers. We have been building things together since we were children. Gausyn is the one we have wanted to build the whole time.

Where we are.

Early. We have a working product, a small group of students using it every week, and a long list of things we want to make better, the depth of the lessons, the speed, how well it remembers you.

We are building it in Toronto, for students everywhere. If you want to be among the first to use it, or you run a school, a program, or a classroom you think it belongs in, we would genuinely like to hear from you.
