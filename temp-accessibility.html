Accessibility

Last updated 18 October 2026gausyn, a product of Inquisara

gausyn is meant to teach every student, so it has to work for students with disabilities. This page says what works today, the standard we build to, where gausyn still falls short, and how to ask for help.

gausyn is in private testing and this page describes it as it is today. It will be reviewed with a lawyer, with the Privacy Policy and the Terms, before gausyn opens to the public. If anything here matters to a decision you are making, write to us and we will answer plainly.

1.Our commitment

We build gausyn to be usable by students with disabilities: students who use a screen reader or a magnifier, who use a keyboard or a switch instead of a mouse, who need larger text or stronger contrast, who cannot draw precisely, who are deaf or hard of hearing, and students who need more time.

We are not finished. This page lists what works and what does not work yet, so that a family or a school can decide with the facts in front of them.

2.The standard we build to

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. It includes the WCAG 2.0 Level AA requirements that Ontario's accessibility law, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), refers to, and it is the level many schools ask for.

gausyn does not meet that target everywhere yet: section 5 lists where it falls short. No independent audit has been done, and we have not published an accessibility conformance report (a VPAT). We plan both before gausyn opens to the public. If your school needs documentation now, write to us and we will tell you exactly where things stand.

3.What works today

  • Speak or type, whichever suits you. Every question can be asked out loud or typed. Nothing in gausyn requires a microphone, a camera or drawing: a whole lesson can be done in text.
  • Everything spoken is also written. In a whiteboard lesson, what gausyn says also appears in the chat. In Learning, replies are written, and voice mode reads them aloud. Students who are deaf or hard of hearing can follow in text, and students who find reading hard can listen.
  • Pictures in Learning have a description. A picture gausyn makes in a Learning conversation carries a short written description that screen readers read out.
  • Reduced motion. If your device is set to reduce motion, gausyn's animations stop and pages appear at once, in their final state.
  • Light and dark. Settings, Appearance, offers Light, Dark, or Match system.
  • Zoom and larger text. The calendar, Learning, Projects and Settings reflow when you zoom the browser or use larger text. The whiteboard is a fixed-size sheet: zooming makes it bigger to move around rather than rearranging it.
  • Your pace, in your words. gausyn teaches at the student's level, and "slower", "say that again", "wait" or "explain it another way" always work.
  • More than one way to do things. A lesson can be moved by dragging it on the calendar, or by asking in the chat on the Home page instead.
  • Phones and tablets. gausyn works with touch as well as with a mouse.

4.Time limits

Some parts of gausyn run on a clock, and we would rather say so plainly.

  • Planned lessons have a set length, chosen when they are planned, and stop when their time is up. A lesson can be made longer on the calendar before it starts, up to 4 hours.
  • Check questions in a lesson gausyn leads (a planned lesson, or one started with the Teacher button). When gausyn asks a check question, it waits 30 seconds for an answer. If none comes, it explains the answer itself, then waits 20 seconds on "Did that make sense?" before moving on. The student can answer, type or ask a question at any moment, including after the time is up.
  • "Wait" holds a lesson. Saying or typing "wait", "stop", "hold on" or "give me a minute" holds the lesson, with no countdown, until the student says "continue" or "keep going".
  • The welcome tour after sign-up moves on by itself. It can be left at any time with "Skip tutorial".
  • Everywhere else, gausyn waits for you. Learning, Projects, the chat on the Home page, and the whiteboard outside a lesson gausyn leads, such as help with your own homework, have no timers.

There is no setting yet to make the waits longer or to turn them off for every lesson, and we plan to add one. Until then, "wait" holds a lesson for as long as a student needs.

5.Known gaps

We would rather list these than let you discover them:

  • The whiteboard is visual. The drawings gausyn makes on the board have no text description yet, and what is on the board is not available to a screen reader. A student who cannot see or use the board can still learn by voice and text, in the lesson chat and in Learning.
  • New replies in a whiteboard lesson are not announced. A screen reader does not yet read a new reply in the lesson chat by itself. gausyn's voice does read it aloud.
  • Keyboard use is incomplete. The board's drawing tools and tabs, dragging lessons on the calendar, and some menus still need a mouse or touch.
  • The waits in lessons (section 4) cannot be made longer in a setting yet. Saying "wait" holds a lesson in the meantime.
  • Scanned worksheets. An uploaded worksheet is shown as it was made. gausyn reads a scanned page to know where its hints should go, but it does not yet pass that text to screen readers.
  • Calls with friends in Collaborate have no captions.
  • Colour contrast has not been checked in every state of every part of gausyn.

6.Assistive technology

gausyn is a web app. It runs in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on computers, phones and tablets.

We have not yet tested it systematically with screen readers such as VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS or TalkBack, or with switch and voice control software. If you use one of these and something does not work, that is exactly the report we want.

7.Asking for an accommodation

If something in gausyn stops a student from learning, write to us and describe what gets in the way. You do not need to tell us about a diagnosis. We will tell you honestly whether we can fix it, work around it, or not yet, and roughly when. If a student needs gausyn's material in another format, ask and we will work out a way with you.

We use what you tell us only to help with that request. It is not added to what gausyn remembers about the student, and it is handled as our Privacy Policy describes.
