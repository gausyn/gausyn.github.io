Trust

Built carefully, because they are students.

Most of our users are young people handing us their homework, their schedules, and their confusion. That is not ordinary data, and we do not treat it as ordinary. Full policy documents ship before public launch; these are the commitments they will encode.

Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to teach: your profile, your schedule, your sessions. Uploaded homework files are summarized and then discarded — the original file is never kept on our servers. We do not sell data, and we never will.

Terms

Plain-language terms of service are being drafted alongside the product and will be published before public launch. The short version: your work is yours, our job is to teach, and neither of us should need a lawyer to understand the deal.

Accessibility

Every spoken lesson has an equal text path — type instead of talk, read instead of listen. The interface respects reduced-motion settings, keeps keyboard focus visible, and is built to be usable on the cheap, small, or old device a student actually has.

Student data

Student data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared beyond what running the service requires. Parents and guardians can request a full export or deletion at any time by writing to gausyninc@gmail.com.

Responsible AI

By design, Gausyn gives hints and never final answers on a student's own work, grounds its checking in solutions it works out first, and runs sessions on a real server-side clock. We evaluate teaching quality continuously, and when the system is not confident, it says so instead of guessing.

Status

The application at app.gausyn.com is operating normally. A public, real-time status page will live here as we approach launch. If something looks wrong right now, tell us: gausyninc@gmail.com.

Trust is a feature. We build it first.
