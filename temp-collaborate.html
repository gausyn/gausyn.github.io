Why the moon doesn’t fall

It does fall. Gravity pulls it toward Earth all the time, but it also moves sideways, about 1 km every second, so it keeps falling past Earth instead of into it.

How a space works

Together, without the noise.

Your own Gausyn

Each person works on their own tab: ask a question, practice, upload a worksheet, or start a lesson with Teacher.

Every tab is its own room

You see and hear the Gausyn on the tab you are on, and nothing from the tabs you are not.

See who is where

Colored dots on each tab show who is on it, and a friend’s own tab carries their color along the top.

Two chat rooms

One for this tab’s conversation with Gausyn, and Friends, just for you and your friends. Gausyn never reads it.

Talk while you work

Mic and Camera sit in the corner of the board. A ring shows who is speaking, and reactions float up with a name.

Friends on your terms

Add a friend by email or friend code. Requests wait until they are accepted, and you can decline or cancel any time.

In full

Everything Collaborate does.

01

Spaces for any group

Make a space, give it a name and a topic, and add the friends you study with. Invite them straight from your friends list, or copy the space’s link from its menu and send it.

02

See who is learning now

Every space shows its members, how many are in it right now, and when it was last active. A live space says so before you open it, on the Collaborate tab and on your Home page.

03

Invites that wait for you

When a friend invites you to a space, the invite waits at the top of Collaborate until you join it or choose Not now.

04

Friends and friend codes

Add a friend by email or with their friend code. You can see which space each friend is in right now, and a request you send waits until it is accepted, or until you cancel it.

05

One board, live

Every stroke appears for everyone as it is drawn, in the writer’s own color, with a named cursor that shows where each friend is working.

06

Your own Gausyn

Everyone has their own Gausyn on their own tab: ask a question, practice, upload a worksheet, or start a lesson with Teacher, each at your own pace.

07

Every tab is its own room

You see and hear the Gausyn on the tab you are on, and nothing from the tabs you are not, so a busy space stays calm.

08

Join a friend’s lesson

When a friend starts a lesson, everyone gets a note with a Join button, and nobody is moved. Open their tab, or tap their face in the people strip, and start listening from wherever the lesson is.

09

Answered by name

On a friend’s lesson tab, ask or answer and their Gausyn replies to the whole group, by name, so everyone hears the question and the explanation.

10

Two chat rooms

One for this tab’s conversation with Gausyn, and Friends, just for you and your friends, with an unread badge. Gausyn never reads the Friends room.

11

Voice, video, and reactions

Turn on your mic or camera from the corner of the board. A ring shows who is speaking, and quick reactions float up with your name without interrupting anyone.

12

Who is where, at a glance

Colored dots on each tab show who is on it, the people strip shows which tab each friend is on, and a friend’s own tab carries their color along the top.
