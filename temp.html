What a rate actually measures

  • How much of something appears or disappears, per unit of time.
  • Always a change divided by an interval, never a single reading.
  • The sign tells you which side of the arrow you are standing on.
rate = Δ[A] / Δt
Lesson · page 2 of 6

Why concentration changes the rate

  • More particles in the same space collide more often.
  • More collisions means more of them clear the energy barrier.
  • Second order in A: doubling [A] quadruples the rate.
rate = k[A]²[B]

Why this needs to exist

The way we learn has not kept up.

Everything a student reaches for today was built to broadcast, not to teach. Lessons that cannot hear you. Courses tuned to an average student who does not exist. Apps that finish the homework and end the thinking. Gausyn exists to close the loop every one of them leaves open: the moment you say wait, I don’t get it.

wait, what?

Recorded lessons play at you

The video cannot hear you. Pause it a hundred times and it says the same thing, the same way, at the same speed. The moment you stop following, you are on your own.

everyone
you

One pace for a million students

Fixed curricula are tuned to the middle of an imaginary class. Nobody lives there. Real understanding slows down, doubles back, then suddenly sprints, and the course should move with it.

x = 4
understanding

Answer apps end the thinking

A photo in, an answer out. The assignment gets finished and nothing gets learned, so tomorrow’s problem is just as impossible as today’s.

$95/hr
two slots left

Great teachers do not scale

The best fix has always been a patient human, one-on-one. But an hour costs more than most families can spend weekly, and the teacher who finally makes it click has thirty other students.
