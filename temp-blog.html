Updates

What is new in Gausyn.

Every time we release a new version of Gausyn, we write down what changed for you in plain language. The newest version is at the top.

Version 0.2 October 2026 · Learning, Projects, and Collaborate

Gausyn grew from one board into four places to learn: Learning for questions between sessions, Projects for the long work, and Collaborate for learning with friends, with the whiteboard where lessons happen at the heart of it.

Learning

A teacher for every subject

A new Learning tab gives every subject its own teacher, there between sessions at any hour. Each one knows your level and your week, and opens a whiteboard session when a question deserves one.

Lessons

Every step drawn beside the words

Lessons now draw each step as they explain it, right beside the words, and wait for the drawing and the voice before asking you anything. Every lesson gets its own tab with its whole topic as the title.

Memory

Gausyn remembers you

Like a teacher who knows you, Gausyn keeps a few short notes as you learn together: how you like things explained, and what tripped you up last time. You can read every note in Settings and forget any one of them, or all of them.

Projects

The work, beside the chat

Projects puts your essay, deck, or build on the right, written live as you watch: real slides, ten themes, pictures drawn for the work, Present mode, and export to PowerPoint, Word, or PDF.

Apps and games

Games that run as they are written

Ask for an app or a game, 2D or 3D, and watch its files appear, then play it right in the Preview. If something breaks, one button sends the error back to be fixed.

3D

Parts you can print

Engineering and hobby projects can design real parts in 3D. Turn the model, check its size in millimetres, drag its measurements to try a new size, and download an STL for a 3D printer.

Effort

Choose how hard it works

An Effort switch in Projects: Low for quick changes, Medium for most work, and High for big builds and tricky bugs. A new project no longer needs a name, and Undo now works for drafts as well as code.

Collaborate

Learn with your friends

A new Collaborate tab for study groups: shared boards in real time, named cursors, everyone’s handwriting in their own colour, voice and video, reactions, and a Friends room Gausyn never reads.

Collaborate

Join a friend’s lesson

Everyone in a space has their own Gausyn on their own tab. When a friend starts a lesson, you get a note with a Join button, and their Gausyn answers the whole group by name.

Friends

Friends and friend codes

Add friends by email or with a friend code, see which space each one is in right now, and answer invites from the top of Collaborate.

Home

Pick up where you left off

Home now shows your latest Learning conversations, your spaces and who is in them right now, and your newest projects, each one tap away.

Plans

Free, Plus, and Pro

Three plans: Free to try Gausyn, Plus for a lesson most days of the week, and Pro for every day, exam weeks, and big projects. Limits reset every five hours and every week, and you can change or cancel from Settings any time.

Settings

Tidier settings, and dark mode

Settings is now organized into tabs, with your plan and usage in one place, and dark mode reaches every part of Gausyn.

Phones

Better on a phone

A pass over the whole app for phones: voices play on iPhones, the tools are compact, and tabs fit the screen.

Honesty

Plain about what it is

Ask Gausyn whether it is a real person and it says plainly that it is not: it is a computer program that teaches.

Version 0.1 August 2026 · First release

The first version of Gausyn that students can sit down and use. It covers the whole loop: planning your week, being taught a lesson, and working through problems on a shared board.

Whiteboard

A board you and your teacher share

Write with a pen, drop in shapes, or upload a worksheet and work straight on top of it. Gausyn sees the page the same way you do, so it can talk about the actual question in front of you instead of a description of it.

Teacher mode

Lessons taught out loud

Pick a topic and Gausyn teaches it page by page, speaking as it goes. Interrupt at any point to ask why, ask for it slower, or ask it to go back. It waits for you rather than running on a timer.

Practice mode

Work the problem yourself

Bring your own worksheet or a photo of your homework. You do the writing; Gausyn watches, and when you stall it offers a nudge or a single step, placed right beside the question you are stuck on. It will not fill in your answers.

Hints

Help that appears where you are looking

Gausyn works out every problem on the sheet for itself the moment you upload it, so when it checks your line four it is checking against real work. Hints stack next to the relevant question rather than scrolling away in a chat window.

Ask Gausyn

More space for a hard question

Drag a box around any problem and it opens in its own workspace, with the original pinned at the top and a clean grid underneath. A marker takes you back to where you were.

Practice questions

Ask for another one

Say you want more practice and Gausyn writes a fresh question on the spot, one at a time. Ask for harder, easier, or just different, and the next one adjusts.

Calendar

A week planned by talking

Tell Gausyn about an exam or an assignment in plain words and it books the study sessions around the hours you are actually free. Change your mind and it re-plans. You can still drag anything by hand.

Deadlines

Exams and due dates you can see

Add a test or an assignment and it sits on the calendar with a live countdown. Gausyn plans the week around it rather than up to it, and leaves the morning of the exam clear.

Sessions

Lessons that are real appointments

A booked lesson opens ten minutes early, runs on a clock kept on our servers, and finishes with a short note about what you covered. Reloading the page or switching devices does not change the time you have left.

Voice

Talk to it, or type

Speak your question and Gausyn answers out loud, or turn the microphone off and type instead. Everything works either way, so a quiet library and a noisy kitchen both work.

Levels

Grade one through graduate school

The same account covers early arithmetic, high school algebra and chemistry, university calculus, and graduate work. The explanations, pace, and vocabulary change with the level rather than the account.

Your files

We do not keep your homework

When you upload a worksheet, Gausyn reads it, writes down a short summary of the topic, and discards the file itself. The summary is what helps it prepare for your next session.

Have something you want Gausyn to do that it cannot do yet? Tell us, and it may well show up in the next version: get in touch.
