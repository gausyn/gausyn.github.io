Kinds of work

Whatever you are making.

Pick a kind and Gausyn shapes its help around it. If none fits, choose Other and it works out what you need.

App or game

2D and 3D games, apps, and tools, written as real files and running live in the Preview as Gausyn builds them.

Presentation

Real slides written live: title, section, bullet, picture, and quote layouts, speaker notes under each, a theme, and Present mode.

Paper or essay

A themed page with a thesis, a structure, and evidence. Never a fabricated citation, and always a pointer to where the real one lives.

Course project

Written coursework, planned and polished: outlines, drafts, tightened arguments, and prose that sounds like you at your best.

Engineering & code

Parts in 3D you can print, circuits, robots, and programs. Testable milestones, code that runs, and safety first.

Hobby build

Plans, steps, and 3D models for something you make. Budget-minded, free tools first, and a cheaper substitute for almost everything.

Other

Anything else you are working on. Gausyn works out what kind of help it needs and adapts: planning, research, writing, building, or practicing.

In full

Everything Projects does.

A live viewer beside the chat

The work sits on the right, large, about sixty percent of the screen by default. Drag the divider to make it bigger, and it remembers where you left it, or press Full width to give it the whole screen. On a phone, Chat and Slides are one tap apart.

Written live, in front of you

Every new version streams in over the old one. The slide or paragraph being written is outlined, what is still to come waits faded, and the view follows the writing. When a version lands, whatever changed glows once and scrolls into view.

Real slides, not a list

Presentations render as true 16:9 slides, with title, section, bullet, picture, and quote layouts that scale with the pane and speaker notes under each one. One idea per slide; the depth lives in your notes.

Pictures, drawn for the work

Where a picture genuinely helps, a title slide, a figure, something easier to see than to read, Gausyn draws one for your project inside a school-safe frame. Pictures start while the rest is still being written, and any of them can be redrawn with one click.

Present from the page

Press Present and the deck goes full screen. Arrow keys or a click move you forward, and you can start from any slide.

Export exactly what you see

Decks download as PowerPoint or PDF, documents as Word or PDF, straight from the draft, with the theme and the pictures in place.

When the scope is vague, Gausyn asks one or two sharp questions first: one cause or several, which sources are allowed, how long the talk runs. The first draft is built for your assignment, not a generic one.
