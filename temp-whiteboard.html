01

Multi-board tabs

Open as many boards as the problem needs. Each tab keeps its own strokes, worksheet, background style, scroll position, and every note Gausyn has left on it. Switch back an hour later and nothing has moved.

02

Upload anything

Multi-page worksheet PDFs render right on the board and you draw directly on top. Photos of homework work too. Pages load as you scroll, so a forty-page packet is as light as a single sheet.

03

It reads the page

The moment a worksheet lands, Gausyn works out where every question, blank, and multiple-choice option sits. That is why its help arrives beside the problem you are on, not in a chat window somewhere else.

04

Hints, never answers

Gausyn writes plain-word nudges, one formula step, or a small drawn diagram, stacked neatly next to the question. It will not fill in your blanks. The pen in your hand is the point.

05

Room when you need it

Drag a box around any problem and it opens as its own workspace with the original pinned on top and a fresh grid below. A pin takes you back to the exact spot you left.

06

Everything persists

Ink, worksheets, hints, and workspaces survive tab switches and the whole session. The board is a record of how you got there, not just where you ended up.

One board, two sets of handwriting.
