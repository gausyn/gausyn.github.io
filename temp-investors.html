The road to Seed

Prove it, then scale it.

Every month we report the same measures to our investors, against these goals. They are goals, not results: Gausyn has not launched yet.

  1. November 2026 to March 2027

    Launch

    A closed beta with the cost of each student measured, a counsel review, then a public launch for ages 13 and up in the US and Canada, and the first paying students.

  2. April to September 2027

    Retention

    Students come back every week through the sessions Gausyn plans for them. Tablets, and families with children under 13 through verified parental consent.

  3. July 2027 to February 2028

    Evidence and schools

    Learning gains measured on delayed tests taken without help, a controlled study under way, and the first school pilots.

  4. February to April 2028

    Seed

    10,000 monthly active students, 500 to 700 of them paying, $200K to $300K in annual recurring revenue, and paid churn of 10% a month or less.
