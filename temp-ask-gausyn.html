01

Crop the problem

Hit Ask Gausyn, drag a rectangle around the question — the diagram, the data table, all of it. That crop becomes the reference for a brand-new workspace tab: Q1, Q2, Q3 as the session grows.

02

Reference pinned, grid below

The workspace opens with your cropped problem pinned at the top and a clean grid underneath. All the explaining, sketching, and second attempts happen down there, at whatever length they need.

03

A pin back home

An anchor stays on the original worksheet, so when the side quest is over you land exactly where you left off — with the workspace still one tab away if you need it again.

04

Hints follow you in

Inside a workspace, Gausyn's help stacks beneath the pinned reference, same rules as everywhere: nudges and steps, never the finished answer.
